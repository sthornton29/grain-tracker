## What this page is for

The Cotton tab is where seed cotton gets tracked from the field to the classed bale. Its left rail follows the cotton's own route: **Seed Cotton Loads** (modules off the field and the gin yard), **Gin Receipts** (the gin's Statements of Ginning), **Bales & Grades** (PBI tags and HVI classing), and **Marketing**. Every page opens the same way — the crop year at the top right, the way in (upload or by hand) in one card with two tabs, and the list below. A user with the **gin operator role** sees only the three intake pages — nothing else in the operation.

## How to use it

- **Seed Cotton Loads**: the page opens on the **season flow** — one bar of this crop year's seed cotton, ginned (green) on the left and still on the yard (amber) on the right, with loads, rolls and pounds per roll beside it and the yard broken out by field underneath so you can see whose modules are still sitting out. Tap "Ginned" or "On the yard" to filter the list to just those loads. Record each module or trailer load in pounds of seed cotton. Enter loads by hand on the **Add by hand** tab (the form remembers your crop year, farm, field and gin; net weight fills in from gross minus tare), or use the **Upload a module list** tab — a photo or PDF of the gin's module list becomes editable rows, one load per page, with the farm matched by FSA number first and producer name second. Review, correct anything, and save the batch. Each load also carries its **rolls** — the number of round modules on it — typed on the form, read from the module list under whatever label the gin prints (Number of Rounds, Rounds, Rolls, Round Modules, Modules), or tapped in later on the load list. **Handwriting is kept apart from print**: when someone wrote a different count on the ticket ("3 Rolls", "Busted Roll, 3 Rolls on TRK #21"), the review row shows the printed count with an amber "handwritten: 3 rolls (…)" chip and a one-tap **Use 3**; the note is saved with the load. Turnrow never picks one over the other for you. A circled or hash-numbered figure in the corner (28, #41) is the farm's own module sequence, shown as a mark and never counted as rolls. The cards at the top total the year's loads, pounds, rolls, and the **average pounds per roll** (total pounds on the loads that have a roll count, divided by their rolls), and the Yard Inventory card shows how many rolls are still waiting. Every load keeps its own **ticket**: the module-list upload stores each page with the load it came from (one load per page — if the page count doesn't match the loads read, every load gets the whole document), the hand-entry form takes a photo or PDF, and a ticket can be added or replaced on the load's page later. The **Yard Inventory** section shows pounds delivered that aren't on any gin receipt yet — your cotton sitting on the yard waiting to be ginned.
- **Gin Receipts**: when the gin sends a Statement of Ginning, enter it here — several can apply to one field. Manual entry works, but the **Upload a Statement of Ginning** card reads the whole document: modules, seed cotton pounds, bales, lint pounds, cottonseed pounds, turnout, the load table, the full bale list across every page, and the gin's Farm Year-To-Date figures. **One PDF can hold several statements** (a 10/10 and a 10/7 statement mailed together): each comes out as its own review card with its own load table, bale check and turnout, and **Save all** saves each as its own receipt. On each card the load lines are matched to your Seed Cotton Loads **by load number with leading zeros ignored** — the module list's 031633 and the statement's 31633 are the same load, and a tick mark the scanner read as a stray character never changes a number. Matched loads are checked against the ticket weights (within 20 lbs is a match; a bigger difference stays linked but turns amber and tells you both figures). When no load number matches — a check mark read as an extra digit, say — Turnrow looks for a saved load with the same net and gross or tare that isn't on any receipt yet and offers it as "Possibly 031641" with a **Link** button; it never links one for you. Only a line nothing answers to offers **Create load**. The footer counts "22 of 22 loads matched · 0 need review · 0 new." When every matched load sits on one farm and field, the receipt defaults to them (chip "from matched loads"); a printed farm number or field that disagrees is flagged, never silently overwritten. Nothing saves until you confirm.
- **Bales & Grades**: import your classing data as a CSV file. Rows are matched to bales by **PBI number** (leading zeros don't matter; a full 12-digit PBI tag matches the gin's 7-digit bale number when its first five digits are that gin's code), a PBI repeated in the file is kept once and named, net weights are cross-checked against the receipt, and rows that don't match a bale are held visibly so you can resolve them after later receipts arrive.

## What the controls do

- **Upload buttons** — every AI upload lands on a review table first; you always confirm before anything is saved.
- **Create missing load** — on gin receipt review, adds a load line the gin has that you never recorded, carrying the rolls count from the statement's load table. It is never offered for a number you already have under another spelling (31633 when 031633 is saved), nor for a number repeated in the statement's own table.
- **Link** — on a receipt line with no number match, links the suggested saved load (same weights, not on any receipt). **Unlink** undoes it before you save.
- **Gin code vs statement number** — "Gin 40614" on a Servico statement is the gin's USDA gin code, not a statement number; both statements in one envelope print it. A receipt is identified by its gin, farm number, field, date and totals unless the gin prints a distinct statement number, and the list shows receipts by gin, date, farm and field. Uploading the same PDF twice flags every statement in it as **Already saved**; two statements with different dates or totals are never treated as duplicates.
- **Farm YTD check** — when a statement prints the gin's Farm Year-To-Date figures, Turnrow compares them with the statements it holds for that gin and farm dated on or before it: green "Farm YTD matches your statements." when they agree, amber "Servico shows 28 modules / 745 bales YTD… Turnrow has 22 / 585. A statement may not be uploaded yet." when the gin has more, and a muted "Gin YTD was printed before this statement posted." when the YTD column is smaller than the statement's own totals. It is information only — it never blocks a save.
- **Rolls** — tap the number in the Rolls column of the load list to change it; Enter saves, Escape cancels.
- **Truck** — the same truck hauls every load, so the truck is remembered from your last load and tucked under **More details** on the hand-entry form (with notes, a new gin name and the ticket photo). It isn't a column in the load list; it still saves with each load, shows on the load's own page as "Hauled on …", and goes out in exports. Change it only when a different truck hauls.
- **Duplicates are caught before they save, whatever the leading zeros.** Every place cotton compares a load number — the module list review, the hand-entry form, the gin receipt's load table — ignores leading zeros and stray tick marks, and the database itself refuses a second load whose number is the same once zeros are ignored (031633 and 31633 can never both be saved for one crop year). In a scan, the same load number on two pages becomes one review row with an "appears twice in this scan (pages 2 and 7)" chip — the first page is its ticket, the second rides along — and two pages that disagree on weight turn red for a look. Against what is already saved, a row is **Already saved** (unticked, with a link), **Update available** (the scan has different weights, dates or rolls — the changes are listed, ticked to apply), or new; the footer counts "X already saved · Y updates · Z new" and only new numbers insert. On the hand-entry form, typing a saved load number shows "Load 031627 is already saved (9/29, 29,860 lbs). Open it?" and the save is blocked unless you tick **Update that load**. On a gin receipt, a bale number repeated in the list or already on another receipt this crop year is named and left out by default (tick to keep), and a receipt number already saved is **Already saved** or **Update available** — nothing is ever saved twice.
- **The load list** works like the grain Loads page: search (load number, farm, field, gin), filters for farm, field, gin, status (on the yard / ginned), and delivered dates; tap a column heading to sort (load numbers sort naturally, blanks stay last); tick loads to export the selection or **Delete selected** — loads already on a gin receipt are never deleted, the confirmation says how many will be kept. **Export** (Excel, PDF, CSV, print) covers the filtered list with the rolls and pounds-per-roll totals.
- **Tap a load** to open its own page: logistics, weights, rolls and pounds per roll, the gin receipt it landed on, notes, and the scanned ticket for that load (view, replace, remove). **Edit** changes any field in place; **Delete** removes it unless it is on a receipt; **Export / Print** makes a one-page record.
- **By field** (under the season bar) — each field's pounds as a bar, ginned in green and on-the-yard in amber, with the pounds and rolls still waiting on the right, and its **yield** on the far right: seed cotton lbs per acre and lint lbs per acre over the field's planted cotton acres (the acres come from the field's planting in Settings → Plantings; a field without one says so). A field reads "all ginned" once every load is on a receipt.
- **Yields everywhere a field or the farm appears.** The season bar's stat row shows the farm-wide seed cotton and lint per acre over the acres that have cotton off them so far (an unpicked field is not a zero); the load's own page shows its field's yield; Gin Receipts shows lint per acre as a card and a "Field lint / ac" column per statement, and the review card quotes what that statement alone makes per acre on the field beside the field's total to date. Lint is **est.** or **part est.** until the gin receipt arrives (tap the chip for the turnout behind it), exactly as on the Yields page — these are the same numbers.

## How the numbers work

- Cotton weights are plain pounds — no moisture or shrink math like grain.
- **Turnout** is lint pounds divided by seed cotton pounds, from the gin receipt.
- Lint yield per acre is the field's bale net weights (from its receipts) divided by planted acres; the app also shows seed cotton pounds per acre alongside.
- **Harvest status comes from the seed cotton loads, ginning status from the receipts.** On the Yields page and the Season Summary a cotton field reads not picked / in progress / complete from its loads — the same rules as a grain field, with seed cotton pounds standing in for bushels — and separately on yard / partly ginned / ginned from which loads have landed on a gin receipt.
- **Lint is estimated until the gin receipt arrives.** Seed cotton on the yard is counted as lint at a turnout percentage and marked **est.** The turnout used is, in order: a figure you set for the crop year, the weighted average of this crop year's gin receipts (lint ÷ seed cotton across all of them), last crop year's, or 40% marked **assumed**. Set your own on the Yields page's cotton table header or under the cotton crop in Marketing → Edit Assumptions; clear it to go back to the automatic figure. Once a field's loads are all on receipts its lint is actual and no turnout is assumed for it.
- Each bale's loan value in cents per pound comes from its classing data and feeds the Bale Quality report and CCC loan figures on the Marketing page.

## Common questions

- **Why is the average per roll blank?** No load in that crop year has a roll count yet. Add rolls on the loads (tap the Rolls figure in the list) and the average appears; loads without a count are left out of the average, and the card says how many.
- **A load shows the whole module list instead of its own page.** The upload read a different number of loads than the document has pages, so it couldn't tell which page was which. Open the load and use **Replace** on the ticket to attach the right page.
- **Why can't the gin operator see Marketing?** The Marketing page is owner-only by design — gin logins get the three intake pages and nothing more.
- **Why don't I see the Cotton tab at all?** Cotton is turned off for your operation. An owner can turn it on under Settings → Organization; the tab appears after the next page load.
- **Why is there no entity box on the load form?** A gin login doesn't pick entities — the entity comes from the farm you choose. Owners see the entity box only when the operation has more than one entity.
- **A classing row didn't match a bale.** Its receipt may not be entered yet — unmatched rows wait visibly and can be matched later.

## If something looks wrong

- Bale-count mismatch flags on a receipt mean the stated total and the captured bale list disagree — recheck the document pages.
- If a load won't match, compare load numbers between the module list and the receipt — leading zeros are ignored, so 031633 and 31633 already count as one. A line that says "two saved loads share this number" means the same load was saved twice under two spellings: open Seed Cotton Loads, keep the one with the module ticket, delete the other, then re-check the receipt.
- A statement that shows **Already saved** when you know it is new: check its date and totals against the saved receipt in the list — a statement is identified by gin, farm, field, date and totals.
- Anything else, contact support.
